The Swarnalok process

Seven steps. Zero secrets.

Everything happens at the counter, in front of you. Stop any time and take your gold back.

  1. Consent & ID

    Sign a simple consent form. Show your PAN and Aadhaar.

  2. Video record

    We film you handing over the jewellery. It protects you and us.

  3. Ultrasonic clean

    Dirt, oil and polish are washed off so only metal is weighed.

  4. Melt to one bar

    Melting blends all metals evenly, so the purity reading is fair.

  5. XRF purity test

    We scan 2 to 4 points. You read the purity on the screen.

  6. Weigh & price

    Certified scale and live market rate. You see the full sum.

  7. Paid to your bank

    Say yes and the money reaches your account at once. No cash.

Good to know

Questions people ask us

What should I bring?

Your gold, your PAN card, your Aadhaar card and your bank account details. A purchase bill helps if you have one, but it is not a must.

Why do you melt the jewellery?

Jewellery has solder, wax and mixed alloys. Melting blends it into one even bar, so the XRF machine reads the real average purity. We melt only after you say yes.

How do I get paid?

Only by bank transfer (IMPS, NEFT or UPI) to an account in your own name. You see the credit before you leave.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. You may stop before melting and take your jewellery back, free. After melting you may still take the bar home without selling.

Why do you record a video?

The video and the consent form are proof that the gold is yours and that the sale was fair. Records are kept private and shared only if the law asks.
