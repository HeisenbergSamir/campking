1. How our price is set

Our buying price starts from the live international gold price, converted to rupees. This price does not include GST, shipping or insurance. We add Indian customs duty on gold and then deduct ₹5,000 per 10 grams of 24K gold. Rates for other purities are worked out in proportion (for example 22K = 91.67% of the 24K rate).

2. What you are paid for

You are paid for the pure gold content: the net weight of the melted bar multiplied by the purity measured by XRF, at the rate in force when we make the offer. Stones, beads, enamel, lac, wax and non-gold metals are not paid for.

3. Website and phone quotes

Rates on our website update automatically every minute and are estimates. Data from price providers can be delayed or briefly unavailable. Quotes by phone or WhatsApp are also estimates. The final offer is made only after testing and weighing at our office, and is valid for 15 minutes.

4. No hidden deductions

We do not charge for testing, cleaning or melting, and we make no wastage deductions other than the material removed in melting, which you see in the before and after weights.

5. Payment

  • Paid only by bank transfer (IMPS, NEFT, RTGS or UPI) to an account in the seller's own name.
  • Usually initiated within minutes of acceptance. Bank processing times may apply.
  • No cash, no cheques to third parties, and no split payments.
  • You receive a signed purchase receipt showing weight, purity, rate and amount.

6. Cancellation

You may cancel at any step before you accept the offer, free of charge. Once you accept and payment is initiated, the sale is final and cannot be reversed.

7. Taxes

Any taxes that apply to a sale are handled as required by law. You are responsible for reporting capital gains in your own tax return where required.
