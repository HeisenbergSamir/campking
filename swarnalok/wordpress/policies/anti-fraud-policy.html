Swarnalok does not buy stolen, pledged or disputed gold. This policy explains how we protect our customers, our business and the public from theft and fraud.

1. What we will not buy

  • Gold that the seller does not own, or cannot show authority to sell.
  • Gold that is pledged, mortgaged, under a loan, or part of a legal or family dispute.
  • Gold from anyone under 18, or from anyone who appears intoxicated or under pressure.

2. How we prevent fraud

  • Full KYC and a signed ownership declaration for every sale.
  • Video recording and CCTV for every transaction.
  • Purity measured by XRF in front of the seller, so neither side can misrepresent the gold.
  • Payment only to the seller's own bank account, never in cash.
  • Regular checks against police and industry alerts for stolen items.

3. If we suspect stolen property

We may stop the transaction, hold the items and records, and inform the police. Handling or receiving stolen property is an offence under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. A person who knowingly offers stolen gold or gives false information may face criminal action.

4. Protection for genuine owners

If you believe your stolen gold may have been offered to us, file a police complaint and ask the police to contact us. We will help with the investigation and share records with the authorities as the law allows.

5. Beware of impersonation

Swarnalok buys gold only at our office in Jakkur, Bengaluru, after an in-person visit. We never ask for advance payments, OTPs or bank passwords, and we never send agents to collect gold from homes unless you booked a visit by calling +91 98153 03553. If someone uses our name differently, report it to us and to the cybercrime helpline 1930.
