1. What we do

We buy old gold jewellery, coins and bars from individuals. We do not sell gold or jewellery, and we do not offer exchange schemes.

2. Who can sell to us

  • You must be 18 or older and the lawful owner of the gold, or hold written authority from the owner.
  • You must show an original PAN card and Aadhaar card, and sell in your own name.
  • Payment is made only to a bank account in your own name.
  • We may decline any transaction without giving a reason, including where documents, ownership or behaviour raise concerns.

3. Ownership and declarations

When you sell, you confirm in writing that the gold is yours, that it is not stolen, pledged, mortgaged or under any dispute, and that the information you give us is true. Giving false information or offering property you do not own is an offence, and we will report it to the police.

4. Our process

  1. Consent and KYC. You sign a consent form and we verify your PAN and Aadhaar.
  2. Recording. The handover and every step after it is video recorded and covered by CCTV. By entering our office you consent to this recording.
  3. Cleaning. Items are cleaned ultrasonically to remove dirt.
  4. Melting. With your written consent, items are melted into a single bar so purity can be measured evenly. Stones, enamel and non-gold parts are removed or burnt off and are not paid for. Melting cannot be reversed. Your jewellery cannot be returned in its original form after melting.
  5. Testing. Purity is measured with an XRF (X-ray fluorescence) analyser at 2 to 4 points, in front of you.
  6. Weighing and offer. The bar is weighed on a calibrated scale and we make an offer based on the live rate.
  7. Payment. If you accept, you sign the sale and we transfer the amount to your bank account.

5. Your right to stop

You may stop at any step before you accept our offer. If you stop before melting, your items are returned as they are. If you stop after melting, the gold is returned to you as a bar. There is no fee for stopping.

6. Prices

Prices shown on our website and quoted by phone or WhatsApp are estimates. The final price depends on the measured purity, the net weight after melting, and the market rate at the time of our offer. An offer is valid for 15 minutes because gold prices move during the day. See our Pricing and Payment Policy.

7. Payment

We pay only by bank transfer (IMPS, NEFT, RTGS or UPI) to an account in the seller's name. We never pay in cash and never pay third parties. A sale is complete when the transfer is initiated and you have signed the purchase receipt.

8. Records

We keep KYC documents, consent forms, purchase records and recordings as required by law, including the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002. See our Privacy Policy for how we protect them.
