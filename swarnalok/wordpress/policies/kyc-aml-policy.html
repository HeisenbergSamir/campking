Swarnalok, a unit of Jumpking International LLP, follows strict Know Your Customer (KYC) and anti-money-laundering (AML) practices to keep gold buying safe and lawful. We follow the Prevention of Money-laundering Act, 2002 and its rules, and income-tax law, as they apply to dealers in precious metals.

1. KYC for every seller

  • Original PAN card and Aadhaar card, checked in person.
  • A live photograph taken at the time of sale.
  • A signed consent form and a declaration of ownership.
  • Bank account details in the seller's own name.

We do not buy gold without complete KYC, whatever the amount.

2. No cash

We pay only by bank transfer to the seller's own account. We do not make cash payments, split payments to avoid limits, or pay any third party.

3. Enhanced checks

We apply extra checks, and may decline or pause a transaction, when:

  • the seller cannot explain how they acquired the gold, or the quantity is unusual for an individual;
  • the same person sells repeatedly within a short period;
  • documents look altered, or the seller refuses KYC or recording;
  • someone else appears to be directing the sale, or the seller seems under pressure;
  • items appear on a police or industry alert for stolen property.

4. Reporting

Where required, we report suspicious transactions to the Financial Intelligence Unit – India and cooperate with law enforcement. We do not tell a person that a report has been made.

5. Records

We keep KYC documents, transaction records and recordings for at least five years from the date of the transaction.

6. Staff

Our team is trained to follow this policy, and a designated officer reviews it at least once a year.
