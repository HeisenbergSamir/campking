Swarnalok, a unit of Jumpking International LLP, respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and how we protect it, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.

1. What we collect

  • Identity: name, photograph, PAN, Aadhaar (stored with the first 8 digits masked), address and date of birth.
  • Contact: phone number, email and the area you visit from.
  • Transaction: description, weight and purity of your gold, the price paid, and your bank account details for payment.
  • Recordings: video and CCTV footage of your visit and transaction.
  • Website: basic technical data such as device and browser type, and messages you send us through WhatsApp or the website.

2. Why we use it

  • To verify your identity and ownership, and to complete and pay for a purchase.
  • To meet legal duties, including KYC, anti-money-laundering, tax and record-keeping requirements.
  • To prevent fraud and the sale of stolen property, and to protect our customers and staff.
  • To reply to your enquiries and arrange visits.

We collect only what we need, with your consent or where the law requires it. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

3. Who we share it with

  • Our bank, to make your payment.
  • Government authorities, regulators and law enforcement, when the law requires it or to report suspected crime.
  • Trusted service providers (for example IT and secure storage), under confidentiality obligations.

4. How long we keep it

We keep KYC and transaction records for at least five years after the transaction, or longer if the law or an investigation requires. Recordings are kept for the same period when linked to a transaction, otherwise for up to 90 days. After that, data is deleted or anonymised.

5. How we protect it

Records are kept in access-controlled systems and locked storage. Only authorised staff can see them. Aadhaar numbers are masked, and documents are never shared on open chat groups.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or update your data, withdraw consent for future use, or raise a grievance. Some records cannot be deleted while the law requires us to keep them. To use these rights, email hello@jumpkingindia.com.

7. Website and WhatsApp

Our website loads live gold rates from third-party price services. When you message us on WhatsApp, your message is also handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
